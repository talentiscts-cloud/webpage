FAQ

Candidate questions, answered plainly

We guarantee the placement process, not an offer letter. Every candidate who clears certification gets a minimum of eight scheduled interviews with hiring partners and stays in the cycle at no extra cost until placed or until they withdraw. Anyone promising a guaranteed job before you've interviewed is guessing.

Two payments. A ₹5,000 registration charge at admission, which is non-refundable, and then 10% of your annual CTC once you're placed. The 10% falls due after your first salary arrives, not on your joining date, and it can be split into flexible EMIs if you prefer. The two are separate: the ₹5,000 is not deducted from the 10%.

On a CTC of ₹4,53,100 the placement fee is ₹45,310, so ₹50,310 in total. If you aren't placed, the 10% never applies and the ₹5,000 is all you have paid. No GST is charged at present. Worked examples are on the programs page.

Not for every track. Compliance-Based Training, QA, Cloud, and Business Analysis accept any graduate from any stream who clears the aptitude test. Full-Stack Java and Data Engineering expect basic programming logic, and our free two-week bridge module covers that if you're starting from zero.

Yes. Every track runs in weekday-evening and weekend formats alongside the full-time day batch, and sessions are recorded for 12 months. Expect 12 to 22 hours a week depending on the track. Placement interviews are scheduled around your availability, though we ask for flexibility in the final two weeks.

Every batch is hybrid: attend on campus or join live online, switching week to week as you need. Two things we ask you to attend in person if you're within travel distance: the capstone review and the mock interview panel days.

The ₹5,000 registration charge is non-refundable. That holds even if you withdraw part-way through a track, so please be sure of the commitment before you pay it. We'd rather talk you out of enrolling on the counselling call than refuse you a refund later.

There is nothing else to refund, because you pay nothing further unless you're placed. The full terms come to you in the enrolment agreement before you pay anything, not after.

No service bonds, and we never hold original certificates. Once you're placed you owe us 10% of your CTC, which is a payment obligation and nothing more. It does not restrict which employer you join, whether you switch, or how long you stay.

You re-enter the next placement cycle at no additional cost, with a fresh set of at least eight interviews, a resume rewrite, two more mock rounds, and refresher access to any module. Full detail, including the 8% who aren't placed in six months, is on the outcomes page.
