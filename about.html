A consultancy that decided to train its own supply

We started on the recruitment side and kept losing mandates for the same reason: the roles were real, the candidates were willing, and the gap between them was six weeks of training nobody was funding. So we funded it.

Our story

Recruiters first, trainers second, and that order matters

Most training institutes add a placement cell once the courses are selling. We went the other way. We spent our first years placing mid-career professionals, which means we know what a hiring manager actually rejects candidates for, and it is rarely the thing the syllabus emphasises.

That experience shapes everything here. Batches are small because mock interviews don't scale. Trainers are working practitioners because candidates get asked about trade-offs, not definitions. And the fee structures put our money at risk alongside yours, because an institute with nothing at stake will happily enrol you into a track with no openings.

We are not the cheapest option and we turn away roughly a third of applicants at the counselling stage. Both of those are deliberate.

How we're structured

  • Training division. Curriculum, instructors, labs, and certification.
  • Placement desk. Recruiters who own candidate outcomes and partner relationships.
  • Employer solutions. Train-to-deploy cohorts, contract staffing, and direct hire.
  • Alumni support. Post-placement check-ins and the second-placement cycle.

The placement desk sits in the same room as the trainers. When partners stop shortlisting from a track, the syllabus owner hears it that week.

What we hold ourselves to

Four commitments we'd rather be judged on

We counsel you out if the odds are bad

If your background, timeline, or target salary doesn't line up with what partners are hiring, we say so on the call. An enrolment we can't place is a refund waiting to happen and a year of your life.

Practitioners teach, not presenters

Every instructor has shipped the thing they teach in a paying job within the last three years. Guest sessions come from hiring managers at partner companies.

We publish the denominator

Placement percentages here are calculated over everyone who certified, not over a filtered subset of "placement eligible" candidates. The counselling call includes the full breakdown by track.

Your data is not a product

Resumes go to the partners you approve, for the roles you approve. We don't sell candidate databases and we don't mass forward profiles to inflate submission counts.
