The train-and-hire model

Five stages from enrolment to offer letter

A typical candidate spends 12 to 16 weeks with us. Here is exactly what happens, and what we hold ourselves to at each stage.

  1. Screen & counsel

    A free aptitude test and a 30-minute call with a career counsellor. We recommend a track based on your background, not on what we want to fill.

  2. Train on live stacks

    Instructor-led sessions with working engineers, daily hands-on labs, and weekly graded assignments. Recordings and mentor office hours included.

  3. Build a capstone

    A production-style project scoped from a real partner requirement, code reviewed by a senior engineer, and shipped to your public portfolio.

  4. Certify & rehearse

    Technical assessment, communication rounds, and at least three recorded mock interviews with written feedback before you meet a single employer.

  5. Place & support

    We schedule the interviews, negotiate the offer, and stay on call through your first 90 days on the job. Didn't click? You re-enter the next placement cycle at no cost.
