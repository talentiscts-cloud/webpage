The other 8%

What happens when a candidate isn't placed

Roughly one in twelve certified candidates is not placed within six months. The reasons cluster into three groups, and we handle each differently rather than quietly dropping the candidate from the report.

  • Interview performance. Free communication clinic and a second placement cycle with fresh partners.
  • Location or salary constraints. We re-scope the target roles with the candidate honestly, including whether the constraint is realistic.
  • Withdrawal. Some candidates take a different path or stop responding. With no placement there is no placement fee, so they owe nothing beyond the registration charge already paid.

The second placement cycle

Included, not sold. If the first cycle ends without an offer you re-enter with:

  • A fresh set of at least eight partner interviews
  • A rewritten resume and portfolio review
  • Two more recorded mock interviews with written feedback
  • Optional refresher access to any module from your track

A second cycle costs you nothing extra. The placement fee only ever applies once you're actually placed, so the delay costs you time rather than money.
